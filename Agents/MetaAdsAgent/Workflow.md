# MetaAdsAgent Workflow

## Purpose

This document defines the operational workflow of MetaAdsAgent.

The agent follows a structured process to analyze, optimize and report Meta advertising performance.

---

# Daily Optimization Workflow

## Step 1 — Data Collection

Collect:

Campaign data:

- Campaign name
- Objective
- Budget
- Spend
- Results
- Purchases
- Revenue

Performance metrics:

- Impressions
- Reach
- Frequency
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- CPA
- ROAS

---

# Step 2 — Performance Analysis

Evaluate:

## Traffic Quality

Check:

- CTR
- CPC
- Engagement

Questions:

- Are users interested?
- Is the creative attracting attention?

---

## Conversion Quality

Check:

- Add to Cart
- Initiate Checkout
- Purchase
- Conversion Rate

Questions:

- Is traffic converting?
- Is the offer strong enough?

---

## Profitability

Check:

- CPA
- ROAS
- Customer Value

Questions:

- Is advertising profitable?
