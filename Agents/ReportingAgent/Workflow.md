# ReportingAgent Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines how ReportingAgent collects, analyzes and presents business information inside Olivos AI OS.

The objective is to deliver timely, accurate and actionable executive reports.

---

# Reporting Workflow

```
Receive Agent Reports

↓

Validate Data

↓

Merge Information

↓

Analyze KPIs

↓

Detect Risks

↓

Identify Opportunities

↓

Generate Report

↓

Deliver Executive Summary
```

---

# 1. Report Collection

Collect reports from:

- CEOAgent
- AnalyticsAgent
- MetaAdsAgent
- SEOAgent
- ContentAgent
- IdeaSoftAgent

Verify:

- Data completeness
- Reporting period
- KPI consistency

---

# 2. Data Validation

Check:

- Missing information
- KPI anomalies
- Duplicate reports
- Reporting accuracy

If inconsistencies exist:

- Flag the issue
- Request clarification
- Continue with verified data

---

# 3. Business Analysis

Evaluate:

## Revenue Performance

- Revenue trends
- Growth rate
- Profitability

## Marketing Performance

- ROAS
- CPA
- CTR
- Campaign efficiency

## SEO Performance

- Organic traffic
- Rankings
- Keyword growth

## Content Performance

- Published content
- Engagement
- Conversion support

## Store Performance

- Store health
- Technical issues
- Customer experience

---

# 4. Risk Detection

Identify:

- Revenue risks
- Traffic declines
- Conversion drops
- Technical problems
- Operational bottlenecks

Assign severity:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

# 5. Opportunity Detection

Highlight:

- Growth opportunities
- Marketing improvements
- SEO wins
- Store optimizations
- Content expansion

Rank opportunities by:

- Business impact
- Ease of implementation
- Expected ROI

---

# 6. Executive Report Generation

Every report includes:

1. Executive Summary

2. KPI Dashboard

3. Key Findings

4. Risks

5. Opportunities

6. Prioritized Action Plan

7. Recommended Next Steps

---

# 7. Distribution

Reports are delivered to:

- CEOAgent
- Business stakeholders
- Management dashboard
- Knowledge Base (archive)

---

# Continuous Reporting Cycle

```
Collect

↓

Report

```

# Core Principle

Reporting is not the final step.
