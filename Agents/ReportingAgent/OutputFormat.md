# ReportingAgent Output Format

## Purpose

This document defines the standard reporting format used by ReportingAgent.

Every report must be consistent, executive-friendly and action-oriented.

---

# Executive Report Structure

```
Executive Summary

↓

KPI Dashboard

↓

Key Findings

↓

Risk Analysis

↓

Opportunity Analysis

↓

Prioritized Actions

↓

Next Steps
```

---

# 1. Report Information

Include:

```
Report Name:

Reporting Period:

Generated Date:

Prepared By:

ReportingAgent

Version:
```

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Provide:

- Overall business status
- Biggest achievement
- Biggest concern
- Immediate priority
- Overall business score (0–100)

Maximum length:

200 words

---

# 3. KPI Dashboard

## Business KPIs

- Revenue
- Profit
- Average Order Value
- Conversion Rate

## Marketing KPIs

- ROAS
- CPA
- CPC
- CTR

## SEO KPIs

- Organic Traffic
- Rankings
- Indexed Pages

## Content KPIs

- Articles Published
- Engagement
- Conversion Contribution

## Store KPIs

- Store Health Score
- Technical Issues
- Customer Experience Score

---

# 4. Key Findings

For each finding include:

```
Finding:

Business Impact:

Evidence:

Priority:

Recommendation:
```

---

# 5. Risk Analysis

Report:

- Risk
- Severity
- Probability
- Business Impact
- Mitigation Plan

Severity:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

# 6. Opportunity Analysis

For each opportunity include:

```
Opportunity

Expected Benefit

Implementation Effort

Estimated ROI

Priority
```

---

# 7. Prioritized Action Plan

## Immediate (0–7 Days)

Critical tasks.

## Short Term (7–30 Days)

Optimization tasks.

## Long Term (30+ Days)

Strategic initiatives.

---

# 8. Executive Recommendations

Every report ends with:

```
Top Priority

Biggest Opportunity

Greatest Risk

Recommended Next Action

Expected Business Outcome
```

---

# Output Rules

Reports must always be:

- Accurate
- Objective
- Executive-friendly
- Actionable
- KPI-driven

Avoid:

- Raw data without interpretation
- Unprioritized recommendations
- Excessive technical detail

---

# Core Principle

The value of a report is measured by the quality of decisions it enables.
