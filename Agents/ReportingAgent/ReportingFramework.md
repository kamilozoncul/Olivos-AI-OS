# ReportingAgent Framework

## Purpose

ReportingFramework defines the reporting standards, KPI evaluation model and executive communication framework used by ReportingAgent.

Its goal is to transform operational data into business decisions.

---

# Reporting Process

```
Collect Reports

↓

Validate Data

↓

Merge Insights

↓

Evaluate KPIs

↓

Identify Risks

↓

Identify Opportunities

↓

Prioritize Actions

↓

Generate Executive Report
```

---

# 1. Data Collection

ReportingAgent gathers reports from:

- CEOAgent
- AnalyticsAgent
- MetaAdsAgent
- SEOAgent
- ContentAgent
- IdeaSoftAgent

Each report must include:

- Current status
- KPI changes
- Problems
- Recommendations

---

# 2. KPI Evaluation

Evaluate:

## Business KPIs

- Revenue
- Profit
- Average Order Value
- Conversion Rate

## Marketing KPIs

- ROAS
- CPA
- CTR
- CPC

## SEO KPIs

- Organic Traffic
- Rankings
- Click Through Rate

## Content KPIs

- Published Content
- Engagement
- Conversion Support

## Store KPIs

- Store Health
- Technical Issues
- Customer Experience

# 3. Risk Assessment

Critical

High

Medium

Low

# 4. Opportunity Assessment

Evaluate opportunities by:

Business Value

+

Growth Potential

+

Implementation Difficulty

+

Time to Impact

Priority:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

# 5. Executive Summary Rules

Every report starts with:

- Overall business status
- Biggest achievement
- Biggest concern
- Immediate priority

---

# 6. Action Planning

Actions are grouped:

## Immediate (0–7 Days)

Critical actions.

## Short Term (7–30 Days)

Optimization actions.

## Long Term (30+ Days)

Strategic improvements.

---

# 7. Reporting Principles

Reports must be:

- Objective
- Data-driven
- Business-oriented
- Easy to read
- Decision-focused
