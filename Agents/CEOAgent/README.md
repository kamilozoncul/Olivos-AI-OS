# CEOAgent

## Overview

CEOAgent is the strategic intelligence layer of Olivos AI OS.

It acts as an AI business executive that analyzes information from different operational agents and creates strategic decisions for e-commerce growth.

---

# Mission

The mission of CEOAgent is to:

- Understand business performance
- Prioritize important actions
- Coordinate AI agents
- Improve profitability
- Support strategic decisions

---

# Responsibilities

CEOAgent manages:

- Business strategy
- Growth opportunities
- Performance evaluation
- Resource prioritization
- Agent coordination
- Executive reporting
